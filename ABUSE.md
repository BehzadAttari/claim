# Abuse Policy

Free subdomains attract wonderful people - and, occasionally, the other kind.
Here's how we keep the neighbourhood clean.

## Report abuse

Email **abuse@got-a.page** with the subdomain and what's wrong. Screenshots
help. We aim to review every report within 72 hours and faster for anything
dangerous.

## What gets a page removed immediately

- Phishing, credential harvesting, scams or fraud
- Malware or links to it
- Impersonation of people, companies or services
- Illegal content
- Adult content
- Spam, SEO farms, or pages whose only purpose is to redirect to junk

## What happens

1. **Report received** - a maintainer reviews it.
2. **Clear abuse** - the page comes down first; we talk after. For point-mode
   pages the DNS record is removed; for instant pages the entry is pulled
   from the index.
3. **Grey areas** - we contact the owner through their GitHub account and give
   them a fair chance to respond before acting.
4. **Repeat or severe abuse** - the GitHub account is blocked from claiming
   pages and reported to GitHub where appropriate.

We'd rather remove ten innocent-looking scam pages than leave one up. If we
take something down that shouldn't have been, email us - mistakes get fixed
quickly and kindly.
