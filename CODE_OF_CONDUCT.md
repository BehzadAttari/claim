# Code of Conduct

got-a.page exists so everyone can have a little home on the web. That only
works if this is a kind place to be.

## How we treat each other

- **Be welcoming.** Many people here are making their very first open-source
  contribution. Remember your first time.
- **Be patient and clear.** Explain things the way you'd want them explained
  to you.
- **Assume good intent.** Most mistakes are just mistakes.
- **Celebrate.** Someone got their page merged? That's wonderful - say so.

## Not here

Harassment, hate speech, personal attacks, trolling, sexualised language,
publishing someone's private information, or using a page to deceive, scam or
impersonate. No exceptions, no "just joking".

## If something happens

Email **abuse@got-a.page**. Every report is read by a maintainer, handled
confidentially, and answered as fast as we can. People who make this place
worse lose their page and, if needed, their access.

This project adapts the spirit of the Contributor Covenant: we commit to a
harassment-free experience for everyone, full stop.
